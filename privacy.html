Tidemori 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective: 2026-11-08

我们如何处理数据

Tidemori 由 chaochen li 提供，用于饮水、用药、用餐和日常节奏记录。应用无需注册专用账号，不包含广告、第三方分析或跨应用追踪。

你输入的计划、饮水、用药、用餐、运动完成记录、心情与笔记保存在设备上，使用 App Group 与本应用的小组件共享，并通过 WatchConnectivity 在配对设备间同步。

登录 iCloud 时，应用会使用你的私人 CloudKit 数据库和 iCloud 键值存储同步应用记录与偏好。这些数据由 Apple 提供的服务处理；开发者不运营接收这些记录的服务器。Apple 健康来源的 HRV 历史仅保存在本机。

Apple 健康与系统权限

只有经你授权，应用才读取 HRV、静息心率、睡眠、心搏间期或你选择的药物信息。经单独启用后，可写入饮水与正念呼吸记录。健康数据不用于广告、营销或数据销售。系统通知及 iOS 26 用药闹钟需要独立授权。

你的控制

你可以在系统设置和健康 App 中管理授权，在应用中编辑或删除支持的记录，并使用导出功能保存副本。删除 App 不会自动删除健康 App 中的样本或 iCloud 中的数据；可在健康 App 与系统 iCloud 存储设置中管理。删除某条应用记录后，删除标记用于防止旧同步消息恢复它。

联系与变更

隐私与支持请求：tjlcccn@foxmail.com。请勿通过邮件发送完整健康记录或敏感医疗信息。政策修改将在本页更新日期。

Data and privacy

Tidemori, provided by chaochen li, helps you track hydration, medications, meals and everyday routines. No dedicated app account is required. The app includes no advertising, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking.

Your plans, water entries, medication and meal logs, exercise completion, reflections and preferences are stored on your devices, shared with the app’s widgets via App Groups, and synchronized between paired devices through WatchConnectivity.

When signed in to iCloud, the app uses your private CloudKit database and iCloud key-value storage to synchronize app records and preferences. Apple processes this data through its services; the developer operates no server receiving these records. HRV history derived from Apple Health stays on-device.

HealthKit access requires your permission. The app may read HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, heartbeat intervals and selected medication information, and optionally write water and mindful breathing entries. Health data is not used for advertising, marketing or sale. Notifications and optional iOS 26 medication alarms require separate permission.

Manage permissions in system Settings and Apple Health. Edit or remove supported app records and export a copy in the app. Deleting the app does not automatically delete Apple Health samples or iCloud data; manage those in Apple Health and iCloud storage settings. Deletion markers prevent old synchronization messages from restoring removed app records.

Contact tjlcccn@foxmail.com for privacy or support. Do not email full health records or sensitive medical information. Policy revisions will update the effective date above.
